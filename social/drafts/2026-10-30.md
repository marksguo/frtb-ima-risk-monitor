Weekly FRTB IMA Risk Desk Recap | Week Ending October 30, 2026

Today's 97.5% Expected Shortfall is 0.0174. This is a baseline measure for potential losses. The stressed Expected Shortfall is 0.0256 and the liquidity-adjusted Expected Shortfall is 0.0353. The significant spread between the current, stressed, and liquidity-adjusted figures indicates heightened market sensitivity and illiquidity premiums.

The current volatility regime remains elevated. This suggests that while the market is moving, the risk profile is still within expected, albeit higher, bounds for this environment. It also implies that our risk capital requirements will likely remain elevated for the time being.

The biggest mover today is GLD, down 3.470%. This move in a key gold ETF suggests either a flight from perceived safe havens or profit taking, which could impact broader market sentiment. I am monitoring its correlation with other assets closely.

The weekly model backtest failed, with an Acerbi-Szekely statistic of -0.5433. However, there were 0 VaR exceptions, which is interesting. This suggests the model is conservative in its VaR estimates but might be overestimating the tail risk slightly or that the chosen statistic is sensitive to current market conditions.

I am watching the implications of the liquidity-adjusted ES being almost double the baseline. This gap, especially in an elevated volatility regime, points to potential challenges in exiting positions quickly without incurring significant costs, which adds extra holding-period penalties for assets that are harder to sell quickly.
